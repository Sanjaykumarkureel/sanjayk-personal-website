01 / Research

The past leaves a trace.
What happens next?

I study the biology of cellular history and adaptation: how past experiences shape a cell’s present state and future response.

Browse publications ↓Google Scholar ↗

A question at the heart of my work

Can we reset a cell’s memory to change its future?

Mechanical, metabolic and immune signals can leave lasting changes in biological systems. I’m interested in how these memories form, when they support adaptation, and when they contribute to dysfunction.
