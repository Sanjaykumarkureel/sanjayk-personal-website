An engineering lens.
A biological curiosity.

I’m an independent researcher exploring biology and society through an engineering lens.

During my doctorate at IIT Bombay, I studied the long-term expansion of human mesenchymal stem cells on soft biomaterial surfaces. My work showed that soft surfaces could maintain stemness and delay replicative senescence.

One finding stayed with me: cells retained aspects of their proliferative and morphological state after moving from a soft surface to tissue culture plastic. That observation led me toward cellular mechanical memory and postdoctoral research on the mechanics of senescence and cell rejuvenation.

My current work explores weight change and mechanical maladaptation, the influence of mechanical adaptation on fibrosis and cellular senescence, and the drivers of longevity and rejuvenation.

I also care about making scientific writing and an understanding of human behaviour more accessible to people whose backgrounds have offered fewer opportunities.

Research skill set

Research experience & skills.

My experimental experience spans stem cell maintenance and characterization, cellular senescence, mechanobiology, and rejuvenation—from biomaterial modulation and cellular assays to imaging and physical performance assessments in mice.

01 / Stem cells & biomaterials

Maintenance and characterization

  • Modulation of biomaterial properties to support stem cell maintenance.
  • Induction and assessment of stem cell differentiation.
  • Stem cell characterization using differentiation assays, surface markers, and senescence assessments.
02 / Cellular function

Functional and phenotypic assays

  • Cell viability and EdU incorporation assays.
  • Cell migration and cell-cycle analysis.
  • Cell morphology assessment and senescence characterization.
03 / Mechanics & imaging

Measuring cells and their behavior

  • Atomic force microscopy (AFM) for cellular mechanical characterization.
  • Traction force microscopy (TFM).
  • Super-resolution microscopy and image analysis.
04 / Cellular pathways

Quality control and cell death

  • Mitochondrial quality-control assays.
  • Autophagy assays.
  • Induction of ferroptosis.
05 / Rejuvenation & memory

Protocol development and mechanical priming

  • Developed a low-frequency, low-intensity ultrasound protocol for rejuvenation of senescent cells.
  • Applied mechanical priming, within the concept of mechanical memory, to reset cellular proliferation and morphology.
06 / In vivo research

Young and aged mouse studies

  • Handling of young and aged mice.
  • Physical performance testing and functional assessments in mice.
