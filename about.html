03 / About me

An engineering lens.
A biological curiosity.

TrainingChemical engineering
DoctorateIIT Bombay, India
Based inHuntsville, Alabama, USA

I’m an independent researcher exploring biology and society through an engineering lens.

During my doctorate at IIT Bombay, I studied the long-term expansion of human mesenchymal stem cells on soft biomaterial surfaces. My work showed that soft surfaces could maintain stemness and delay replicative senescence.

One finding stayed with me: cells retained aspects of their proliferative and morphological state after moving from a soft surface to tissue culture plastic. That observation led me toward cellular mechanical memory and postdoctoral research on the mechanics of senescence and cell rejuvenation.

Today, I’m developing projects on the potential role of mechanosignaling in obesity, weight change and cellular senescence.

I also care about making scientific writing and an understanding of human behaviour more accessible to people whose backgrounds have offered fewer opportunities.
