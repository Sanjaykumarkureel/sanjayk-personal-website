02 / Perspectives

Beyond the laboratory.
The questions connect.

My interests extend from biological systems to the social environments we inhabit and the words we use to understand them.

Human behaviour

Understanding people
in context.

Our relationships, resources and positions within social structures shape how we act. I explore these patterns to ask better questions about the world around us.

Questions I’m exploring +
  • How do security, interests and relationships influence behaviour?
  • How does our position within a power structure change the way we act?
  • Can understanding these patterns help us navigate social life more thoughtfully?

Scientific writing

Better writing.
Clearer thinking.

Writing is more than communicating results. It helps us organize our thoughts, notice gaps in our reasoning and develop ideas—especially when working in a second language.

Questions I’m exploring +
  • How does putting an idea into words sharpen our thinking?
  • What makes a grant, manuscript or review clear and persuasive?
  • How can non-native English speakers build confidence and precision in scientific writing?
